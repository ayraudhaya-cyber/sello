# Sello User Guide

A simple guide for Owners, Managers, and Sales Reps.

This guide matches how Sello works today. If something is not in this guide, the app does not support it yet.

---

## 1. Getting started

### What Sello is

Sello is a **business operating system** for selling, collecting money, and running the team.

It is not a cash register only. It helps you:

- visit customers
- take orders
- deliver goods
- collect money
- follow cheques
- see what customers still owe

### Two workspaces

| Role | Where they work | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Hub (desktop / tablet) | Run the business: orders, customers, payments, team, reports, settings |
| **Manager** | Same Hub as Owner | Same screens as Owner. Some company settings are view-only |
| **Sales Rep** | Sales app (phone / tablet) | Visit shops, take orders, collect money |
| **Store In-charge** | Hub | Stock, products, and delivery-related order work |
| **Sales In-charge** | Hub | Orders, customers, and some payments (cannot approve collections) |

A person signs in with **one** Sello account. That account is tied to **one** Sales Rep or Hub user. You cannot switch “who made this sale” after it is saved. See [Wrong Sales Rep account](#q-wrong-sales-rep).

### Basic navigation

**Sales app (Sales Rep)**

Bottom of the phone (or side menu on tablet):

1. **Home** — today’s plan, nearby customers, continue a visit
2. **Customers** — find a shop and start a visit
3. **Products** — look at the catalog (this does not create an order)
4. **Orders** — see previous orders and drafts
5. **Profile** — your name, company, Help, sign out

**Hub (Owner / Manager)**

Left menu:

- **Dashboard** — snapshot of the business
- **Reports**
- **Orders**, **Inventory**, **Products**, **Suppliers**, **Customers**
- **Payments** (this includes a **Cheques** tab)
- **Schedule**, **Visits**
- **Team**, **Attendance**
- **Settings**

There is **no** separate Approvals page. Collections waiting for approval appear on **Payments**.

There is **no** separate Cheques page. Open **Payments**, then tap **Cheques**.

## 2. Sales Rep — daily work

### Start a visit

1. Open **Home** or **Customers**.
2. Tap the customer.
3. Sello opens the visit workspace.

Other ways:

- **Visit a shop** on Home
- **Walk-in** for a buyer who is not on the list yet
- **Today's plan** if your manager gave you stops for today

There is no separate “Check in” button. Opening the visit **starts** it.

**Important:** Finish or leave your current visit before starting another one (including a walk-in).

### Find / select a customer

Do this **before** or as you start the visit.

You cannot change the customer in the middle of a visit basket.

For a **walk-in**, you add products first. When you submit, Sello asks for:

- Business name
- Phone number
- Contact person
- Area / suburb

Tap **Save & continue**.

### Create an order

During a visit:

1. Add products from the catalog.
2. Change quantities with **+** / **−** or **Enter quantity**.
3. If a product has options (size, colour, pack), tap **Choose options**.
4. Tap **Continue**.
5. Choose how the customer will pay (see below).
6. Ask the buyer to **sign**.
7. Tap **Submit order**.

On **Orders**, you can also tap **New order**, then **Submit order**.

### Save a draft

During a visit, Sello keeps the basket on **this device**.

- Banner: **Draft order** / **Continue order**
- If you leave: **Your progress has been saved.**
- Home may show **Visit in progress** → **Continue**

On desktop Hub, **Save for later** stores a **Draft** order.

A draft is **not** a real sale yet. It does not change stock. It does not change what the customer owes.

### Submit an order

Tap **Submit order**.

What Sello does:

- Saves the order as **Placed**
- Does **not** reduce stock yet
- Does **not** add the order to the customer’s **Outstanding** yet
- May offer to send an order message (SMS / WhatsApp), depending on company settings

**What happens next:** Record delivery when goods go out. Collect money when the customer pays.

### Choose payment arrangement (visit checkout)

This is labelled **Collection**. It is the **plan**, not always the money itself.

| You tap | Meaning | What Sello does |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid today** | Collect money now | After submit, **Receive payment** opens |
| **Credit** | Customer will pay later | Order is saved unpaid. No payment is created |
| **Cheque received** | Customer is giving / has given a cheque | Order is saved unpaid. **Record cheque** may open. You can skip it |
| **Cheque later** | Customer will give the cheque later | A note only. No cheque is created |
| **Arrange later** | Settle later | Order is saved. Collect later from the order |

**Important**

- Choosing **Cheque received** does **not** create a cheque by itself. You must fill **Record cheque** and save. Skipping is allowed.
- **Cheque later** never creates a cheque.

### What Credit means

**Credit** means: the customer takes goods now and pays later.

Sello still saves the order as unpaid.

The customer’s **Outstanding** goes up when goods are **delivered**, not when you tap Submit.

### What Cheque received means

The customer intends to pay by cheque.

Next, you may record cheque details. If you skip, record it later from the **order**.

### What Cheque later means

The customer will give the cheque another day.

You may add an **Expected collection date**. It is optional. It is only a reminder note.

### What happens after submitting an order

1. The visit is finished.
2. You see **Visit saved.** (or a short next-step message).
3. You return to **Home**.
4. The order appears under **Orders** with status **Placed**.
5. Stock still has the goods until someone records **delivery**.
6. Customer Outstanding still shows the **old** amount until delivery (see [the Rs. 2,000 + Rs. 3,000 example](#q-outstanding-not-updating)).

### Record a payment (collect money)

See [How to collect money](#how-to-collect-money).

### Record a cheque

From a visit, use **Cheque received**, then save **Record cheque**.

Or open the **order** → **Record collection** → **Cheque**.

Sales Reps do not have a Payments page. Owner/Manager see the cheque under **Payments → Cheques** automatically. Do not add it again.

### View previous orders

Open **Orders**. Tap an order to see details, delivery, and collections.

### Continue a saved draft

- Home: **Visit in progress** → **Continue**
- Visit banner: **Continue order**

### What happens when offline

| Action | Offline |
|---|---|
| Start / finish a visit | Saved on this device, then sent when you are online |
| Order basket (draft) | Saved on this device |
| Submit order | Needs internet |
| Receive payment | Needs internet |
| Record cheque | Needs internet |

You may see: **Offline · Changes saved on this device**.

## 3. Payments and collections

### How to collect money {#how-to-collect-money}

**Sales Rep — against one order**

1. Open **Orders**.
2. Tap the order.
3. Tap **Record collection**.
4. Check **Outstanding** (what is still unpaid on this order).
5. Enter **Amount received**.
6. Choose **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank**, or **Cheque**.
7. Tap **Record collection**.

**Sales Rep — against a customer (any unpaid orders / opening balance)**

1. Tap **Quick actions**.
2. Tap **Receive Payment**.
3. Select the customer.
4. Tick the orders or opening balance you are collecting. Or tap **Select all**.
5. Check the amount.
6. Choose **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank transfer**, **Wallet**, or **Credit settlement**.
7. Tap **Confirm payment**.

**Owner / Manager**

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Tap **Receive Payment**.
3. Same steps as above.

Cheque is **not** a method inside **Receive payment**. For a cheque, use **Record collection** on the order, or **Payments → Cheques → Record cheque**.

### What happens after you save

If your company **auto-approves** collections (default):

- The payment is **Completed**
- The customer’s Outstanding usually goes **down** straight away

If your company requires **approval**:

- Sales Rep collections stay **Pending Review**
- Outstanding does **not** change yet
- Owner or Manager opens **Payments**, reviews it, then **Approve** or **Reject**
- Outstanding changes only after **Approve**

Owner/Manager collections apply immediately even when approval is required for Sales Reps.

### Opening balance collections

If a customer already owed money before Sello (or you added an opening balance later):

- It appears in **Receive payment** like an invoice, labelled **Opening balance**
- Tick it like any other unpaid item
- Collecting it reduces Outstanding
- It does not create a new order

### Cheque collections

See [Cheques](#5-cheques).

If this visit already has a cheque for the order, **Record collection** may offer:

- **Mark cheque as received** (you now have the paper cheque)
- **Apply existing cheque** (the cheque is already in hand; put leftover amount on this order)

That avoids creating a second cheque by mistake.

## 4. Customers and outstanding balances

### Words Sello uses

| Word | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| **Outstanding** | How much this customer still owes you |
| **Wallet** | Extra money sitting on the customer’s account (the business owes this back as store credit) |
| **Credit allowed** | This customer may buy now and pay later |
| **Credit limit** | A number you record as guidance. Sello **does not currently stop** an order if it goes above this limit |
| **Opening balance** | Money they already owed when you started using Sello, or an extra amount you add later |
| **Credit owing** (Payments card) | Outstanding **only** for customers with credit allowed |

### What increases Outstanding

- Recording **delivery** on an unpaid order
- Adding an **opening balance**
- A cheque that **bounces** or is **cancelled** after it had already reduced the balance

### What reduces Outstanding

- A **completed** collection (cash, card, bank, wallet, credit settlement)
- A cheque marked **received** (in hand), after it is completed / approved
- Owner/Manager **Approve** on a pending collection

### When an order becomes part of Outstanding

When goods are **delivered**.

Saving or submitting an order does **not** add it to Outstanding.

### Why the balance may not change immediately

Common reasons:

1. The order is **Placed** but not delivered yet.
2. The collection is **Pending Review**.
3. You are looking at an old screen — pull to refresh or open the customer again.
4. Sales Reps may be hidden from outstanding amounts if Settings turn that off.

### Outstanding vs Credit owing

On **Payments**:

- **Outstanding** = everyone who still owes money
- **Credit owing** = the part of that total that belongs to customers **allowed to buy on credit**

The gap is unpaid amounts on customers **without** credit allowed.

**Wallet** is separate. Wallet is money the **customer can spend**, not money they owe.

## 5. Cheques

### Record cheque

Use this for a **real cheque in Sello’s cheque book**.

- Sales: after **Cheque received**, or from the order
- Owner/Manager: **Payments → Cheques → Record cheque**

If you already have the paper cheque, leave **I already have this cheque** on.

If the customer has not given it yet, turn that off. Status will be **Waiting to receive**. Outstanding does not change until you later mark it received.

### Add existing cheque

Use this **only** for an old cheque from **before Sello** (tracking).

- Owner / Manager only
- Does **not** change Outstanding
- Does **not** create a payment
- Does **not** collect money
- Do **not** use this for a cheque a Sales Rep just received

### Cheque statuses

| Status you see | Meaning | Money |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting to receive** | Customer has not given the cheque yet | No change |
| **In hand** | You have the cheque | Creates a payment. Outstanding may go down (or wait for approval) |
| **At the bank** | You took it to the bank | Status only |
| **Bank paid** | Bank paid it | Status only |
| **Bank returned** | Bounced | Outstanding goes back up if it had been applied |
| **Cancelled** | Will not be used | Same reverse if it had been applied |

Owner/Manager move a cheque forward: mark received → take to bank → bank paid it.

### Where Sales Rep cheques appear

They appear automatically under **Hub → Payments → Cheques**.

The Owner does **not** add them again.

You will see the customer, amount, dates, and links to the payment when the cheque is in hand.

### If approval is required

A Sales Rep cheque that is in hand may show **Received — needs approval**.

Outstanding changes after the Owner/Manager approves it.

### After an order, if you skipped the cheque

Open the **order** → **Record collection** → **Cheque**.

## 6. Owner / Manager — daily operations

### Dashboard

A quiet snapshot: sales, customers, outstanding, low stock.

Tap a card to open that workspace.

### Orders

See every order. Open one to:

- **Record delivery** / **Deliver remaining** / **Mark as delivered**
- **Record collection**
- View invoice, WhatsApp, SMS (if allowed)
- Cancel remaining or archive completed orders

**Mark as delivered** finishes remaining goods, reduces stock, and updates Outstanding for unpaid delivered value.

### Customers

Add customers, credit allowed, opening balance, and later **Add opening balance**.

### Payments

Two tabs:

- **Payments** — money received. Use **Receive Payment**
- **Cheques** — cheque book. Use **Record cheque** or **Add existing cheque**

Yellow banner: **collection awaiting review** — approve or reject there.

### Products / inventory

Products are the catalog. Inventory is stock on hand.

Sales can order above stock only if Settings allow **Allow orders beyond available stock**.

### Team

People, roles, and who can sign in.

### Reports

See [Reports](#7-reports).

### Settings (high-value)

**Orders & Invoices**

- Whether Sales Reps can see outstanding
- Whether Sales Rep collections need approval
- Whether Sales Reps can record delivery
- What the customer invoice may show

**Inventory**

- Allow orders beyond available stock

**Notifications**

- Order confirmation, collection messages
- SMS quota (only if your company has an SMS pack)

**Product Details**

- Extra product fields for your industry

Owner and Manager share this Hub. Manager cannot change some company settings.

## 7. Reports

The full export that is ready today is **Collections Report**.

### What “As of” means

The report answers: “On this date, what was still unpaid?”

It uses the **end of that day**.

Example: As of 31 March includes everything that happened on 31 March.

### Sales Rep filters

Tap Sales Rep chips. Empty selection = **All Sales Reps**.

This uses the Sales Rep stored on each **order** (the person whose account created it).

### Opening balances in reports

Opening-balance rows appear when you have **not** filtered to one Sales Rep.

They are not tied to a Sales Rep.

### Exporting

On Collections Report: **Export PDF** and **Export Excel**.

## 8. Common questions

### Sales Rep

#### Can I change the Sales Rep on an order? {#q-wrong-sales-rep}

**No.** Not the Sales Rep, and not the Owner/Manager.

The order keeps the person who was signed in when it was created.

**What to do**

1. If it is still a **Draft**, cancel it and create it again while signed in to the **correct** account.
2. If it is already **Placed**, do not recreate money twice. Tell the Owner. Keep the order. Use reports knowing it sits under the wrong name, or follow your company’s correction process (cancel remaining if goods were not delivered, then create the correct order).

Sello does not have “reassign Sales Rep” on an order.

#### How do I collect money from a customer?

See [How to collect money](#how-to-collect-money).

Outstanding changes when the payment is **Completed** (or when the Owner **approves** it). It does not change while status is **Pending Review**.

#### Customer owed Rs. 2,000. I made a Rs. 3,000 credit order. Why is Outstanding not Rs. 5,000? {#q-outstanding-not-updating}

Because submitting an order is **not** the same as delivering it.

| Step | Outstanding |
|---|---|
| Before | Rs. 2,000 |
| After **Submit order** (Credit) | Still **Rs. 2,000** |
| After **Record delivery** of the Rs. 3,000 (still unpaid) | **Rs. 5,000** |

**Where to check**

- Open the **order**
- Status should be **Placed** (or **Partially delivered**)
- **Delivery** shows Ordered / Delivered / Remaining

If Remaining is not zero, this new order is not fully on Outstanding yet.

If your company does not allow Sales Reps to record delivery, the Owner/Manager must tap **Record delivery**.

---

### Owner / Manager

#### A Sales Rep recorded a cheque. Where is it?

**Payments → Cheques.** It appears by itself.

Do **not** use **Add existing cheque** for it.

If it is **Waiting to receive**, Outstanding has not changed yet.

If it is **In hand** and approval is on, approve it on Payments / the cheque. Then Outstanding changes.

#### Why is Record cheque on Payments and on Cheques?

They were the same action. **Record cheque** now lives on the **Cheques** tab, which is the cheque book.

**Payments** is for money received (**Receive Payment**).

#### What is Add existing cheque?

A **history note** for a cheque from before Sello.

It does not collect money. It does not change Outstanding. Only Owner/Manager/Admin can add one.

### More questions

**Draft vs submitted**  
Draft = not a sale yet. Placed = order is real, delivery may still be pending.

**Paid vs Credit vs Cheque**  
Paid today = collect now. Credit = pay later. Cheque = paper cheque, recorded separately.

**Can I order more than stock?**  
Only if Settings allow it. Otherwise Sello blocks submit.

**Product options**  
They are choices on a product (for example size). Pick them when adding the line.

**Wallet vs Outstanding**  
Outstanding = they owe you. Wallet = you hold extra credit for them.

**SMS quota**  
Shown if your company has an SMS pack. A failed SMS never undoes an order or collection.

**Notifications**  
Owner/Manager may get an in-app inbox item when an order is completed or a collection is submitted.

## Important limits (so nobody is surprised)

Sello **does not** currently let you:

- Change the Sales Rep on an existing order
- Automatically block sales above credit limit
- Submit orders, payments, or cheques while offline
- Use WhatsApp Business API (device share only)
- Open a separate Approvals or Cheques menu
