# Sello User Guide

A simple guide for Owners, Managers, and Sales Reps.

This guide matches how Sello works today. If something is not in this guide, the app does not do it yet.

---

## 1. Getting started

### What Sello is

Sello helps you run sales, money, stock, and the team.

You can:

- visit customers
- take orders
- deliver goods
- collect money
- follow cheques
- see what customers still owe

### Who uses which app

| Role | Where they work | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Hub (computer or tablet) | Run the business |
| **Manager** | Same Hub as Owner | Same screens. Some company settings are view-only |
| **Sales Rep** | Sales app (phone or tablet) | Visit shops, take orders, collect money |
| **Store In-charge** | Hub | Stock, products, and delivery |
| **Sales In-charge** | Hub | Orders, customers, and some payments. Cannot approve collections |

One person uses one Sello account. That account is one person in the team.

### How to move around

**Sales app**

Bottom of the phone (side menu on a tablet):

1. **Home** — today’s plan and the visit you are in
2. **Customers** — find a shop and start a visit
3. **Products** — look at the catalog. This does not create an order
4. **Orders** — past orders and drafts
5. **Profile** — your name, Help, sign out

**Hub**

Left menu:

- **Dashboard**
- **Reports**
- **Orders**, **Inventory**, **Products**, **Suppliers**, **Customers**
- **Payments** (money, and a **Cheques** tab)
- **Schedule**, **Visits**
- **Team**, **Attendance**
- **Settings**

There is no separate Approvals page. Collections waiting for approval are on **Payments**.

There is no separate Cheques page. Open **Payments**, then **Cheques**.

### When a new version opens

The first time you open a new version, Sello may show **What’s new**.

Tap **Nice**, the close button, or the area outside the card. That closes it.

### Clear filters

When a search or a filter is on, **Clear filters** appears on that filter row.

Tap it to go back to the normal list.

The usual **Active** filter on Products and Customers is the normal list. It does not show **Clear filters**.

On Schedule, **Clear filters** does not change the calendar view or the date you are looking at.

## 2. Sales Rep — daily work

### Start a visit

1. Open **Home** or **Customers**.
2. Tap the customer.
3. The visit opens.

Other ways:

- **Visit a shop** on Home
- **Walk-in** for a buyer who is not on the list yet
- **Today's plan** if your manager gave you stops for today

There is no separate Check in button. Opening the visit starts it.

Finish or leave the current visit before you start another one.

### Find a customer

Do this before the basket is under way.

You cannot change the customer in the middle of a visit basket.

For a **walk-in**, add products first. When you submit, Sello asks for:

- Business name
- Phone number
- Contact person
- Area / suburb

Tap **Save & continue**.

### Create an order

During a visit:

1. Add products.
2. Change quantities with **+** / **−** or **Enter quantity**.
3. If a product has options (size, colour, pack), tap **Choose options**.
4. Tap **Continue**.
5. Choose how the customer will pay.
6. Ask the buyer to **sign**.
7. Tap **Submit order**.

On **Orders**, you can also tap **New order**, then **Submit order**.

### Save a draft

During a visit, Sello keeps the basket on **this phone**.

- Banner: **Draft order** / **Continue order**
- If you leave: **Your progress has been saved.**
- Home may show **Visit in progress** → **Continue**

On the Hub computer, **Save for later** stores a **Draft**.

A draft is not a sale yet. It does not change stock. It does not change what the customer owes.

### Submit an order

Tap **Submit order**.

Sello then:

- saves the order as **Placed**
- does not reduce stock yet
- does not add the order to **Outstanding** yet
- may offer to send a message (SMS or WhatsApp), if your company turned that on

Next: record delivery when the goods go out. Collect money when the customer pays.

### Payment plan at checkout

This is labelled **Collection**. It is the plan. It is not always the money itself.

| You tap | Meaning | What Sello does |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid today** | Collect money now | After submit, **Receive payment** opens. It selects the order you just placed |
| **Credit** | Customer will pay later | Order is saved unpaid. No payment is created |
| **Cheque received** | Customer is giving a cheque | Order is saved unpaid. **Record cheque** may open. You can skip it |
| **Cheque later** | Customer will give the cheque later | A note only. No cheque is created |
| **Arrange later** | Settle later | Order is saved. Collect later from the order |

**Cheque received** does not create a cheque by itself. Fill **Record cheque** and save. You may skip it.

**Cheque later** never creates a cheque.

### What Credit means

The customer takes the goods now and pays later.

**Outstanding** goes up when the goods are **delivered**, not when you tap Submit.

### After you submit

1. The visit is finished.
2. You see **Visit saved.**
3. You return to **Home**.
4. The order is under **Orders**, status **Placed**.
5. Stock still has the goods until someone records **delivery**.
6. Outstanding stays the same until delivery. See [the Rs. 2,000 example](#q-outstanding-not-updating).

### Collect money and cheques

See [How to collect money](#how-to-collect-money) and [Cheques](#5-cheques).

### Previous orders

Open **Orders**. Tap an order to see details, delivery, and collections.

Pull down to refresh **Home**, **Customers**, **Products**, and **Orders**. Keep scrolling to load more.

### Continue a draft

- Home: **Visit in progress** → **Continue**
- Visit banner: **Continue order**

### Cancel a draft

A Sales Rep can cancel **their own draft**.

Open the draft and tap **Cancel order**. Stock and the customer balance stay the same.

A Sales Rep cannot cancel an order after it is placed. Ask the Owner or Manager.

### Without internet

| Action | Offline |
|---|---|
| Start or finish a visit | Saved on this phone, then sent when you are online |
| Order basket (draft) | Saved on this phone |
| Submit order | Needs internet |
| Receive payment | Needs internet |
| Record cheque | Needs internet |

You may see: **Offline · Changes saved on this device**.

Product photos you already opened stay on the phone.

## 3. Payments and collections

### How to collect money {#how-to-collect-money}

**On one order (Sales Rep, Owner, or Manager)**

1. Open the order.
2. Tap **Record collection**.
3. Check what is still unpaid.
4. If the customer has wallet credit, **Apply from wallet** is filled for you. You can change it.
5. **Amount still to receive** is the rest. Choose **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank**, or **Cheque**.
6. Tap **Record collection**.

One save records both parts.

The wallet amount cannot be more than the customer’s wallet, and it cannot be more than what is still due.

If the wallet covers the whole amount, you can leave the rest at zero.

**Against several orders, or an opening balance**

Sales Rep: **Quick actions** → **Receive Payment**.

Owner or Manager: **Payments** → **Receive Payment**.

1. Select the customer.
2. Tick the orders or the opening balance. Or tap **Select all**.
3. Check the amount.
4. Choose **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank transfer**, **Wallet**, or **Credit settlement**.
5. Tap **Confirm payment**.

On this screen, **Wallet** is the whole payment. It is not mixed with cash on the same save. To mix wallet with cash, card, bank, or cheque, use **Record collection** on the order.

Cheque is not a method inside **Receive payment**. For a cheque, use **Record collection** on the order, or **Payments → Cheques → Record cheque**.

### What the wallet is

Wallet is extra money already sitting on the customer’s account. They can spend it later.

It is not money they owe you. Outstanding is the opposite: money they still owe you.

If they pay more than the invoices you ticked, the extra goes into the wallet. Sello does not hand that extra back as cash.

See [Outstanding and wallet, by role](#outstanding-and-wallet-by-role).

### After you save

If your company applies collections straight away (this is the usual setting):

- The payment is **Completed**
- Outstanding usually goes **down** at once

If Sales Rep collections need approval:

- The collection stays **Pending Review**
- Outstanding does **not** change yet
- Owner or Manager opens **Payments**, then **Approve** or **Reject**
- You can approve several at once
- Outstanding changes only after **Approve**

Owner and Manager collections apply at once, even when Sales Rep collections need approval.

A failed SMS never undoes an order or a collection.

### Opening balance

Money the customer already owed before Sello, or an amount you add later.

- On a new customer, fill **Opening balance** when you add them
- Later, open the customer and choose **Add opening balance**
- It raises Outstanding. It does not create an order
- In **Receive payment** it appears as **Opening balance**. Tick it like any other unpaid item

### Cheque on the same visit

If this visit already has a cheque for the order, **Record collection** may offer:

- **Mark cheque as received** — you now have the paper cheque
- **Apply existing cheque** — the cheque is already in hand. Put any leftover amount on this order

That stops you creating a second cheque by mistake.

## 4. Customers and outstanding balances

### Words Sello uses

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Outstanding** | How much this customer still owes you |
| **Wallet** | Extra money on their account. They can use it on a later collection |
| **Credit allowed** | This customer may buy now and pay later |
| **Credit limit** | A number you keep for guidance. Sello does not stop an order that goes over it |
| **Opening balance** | Money they already owed, or an extra amount you add later |
| **Credit owing** | Outstanding only for customers who are allowed to buy on credit |

### How the two balances work

Think of two separate numbers on each customer.

**Outstanding** goes up when you deliver goods they have not paid for, or when you add an opening balance. It goes down when a collection is completed (or approved). Submitting an order does not change it.

**Wallet** goes up when the customer pays more than the invoices you ticked. It goes down only when someone applies that credit on a collection. It never becomes cash in the till by itself.

They do not cancel each other out. A customer can owe Rs. 12,000 and also have Rs. 3,000 in the wallet. You apply the Rs. 3,000 when you collect, and they still pay the rest.

Sello does not pull wallet in by itself when you raise an invoice. You choose it at collection time.

### Outstanding and wallet, by role {#outstanding-and-wallet-by-role}

| | Owner / Manager | Sales In-charge | Sales Rep | Store In-charge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See outstanding in the Hub | Yes | Yes | No Hub app | Can open customers. No Payments page |
| See outstanding in the Sales app | — | — | Only if **Orders & Invoices** allows it. Home and the visit hide it when that is off | — |
| See wallet | Yes, on the customer | Yes, on the customer | On **Record collection**, when the customer has wallet credit | No collection screen |
| Apply wallet with cash, card, bank, or cheque | **Record collection** on the order | Same | Same | No |
| Use wallet as the whole payment | **Receive Payment** | Same | **Quick actions → Receive Payment** | No |
| Approve a pending collection | Yes | No | No | No |
| Correct a payment, invoice, opening balance, or cheque | Yes | No | No | No |

A pending Sales Rep collection does not change outstanding or wallet until an Owner or Manager approves it. Owner and Manager collections apply at once.

The customer invoice shows outstanding only if Settings say the customer copy may include it. Wallet is not printed as money they owe.

### What raises Outstanding

- Recording **delivery** on an unpaid order
- Adding an **opening balance**
- A cheque that **bounces** or is **cancelled** after it had already reduced the balance

### What lowers Outstanding

- A **completed** collection (cash, card, bank, wallet, or credit settlement)
- A cheque marked **in hand**, after it is completed or approved
- An Owner or Manager tapping **Approve** on a pending collection

### When an order joins Outstanding

When the goods are **delivered**.

Saving or submitting an order does not add it yet.

### If the balance looks wrong

1. The order is **Placed** but not delivered.
2. The collection is **Pending Review**.
3. The screen is old. Pull down to refresh, or open the customer again.
4. Settings hide outstanding from Sales Reps.

### Outstanding and Credit owing

On **Payments**:

- **Outstanding** = everyone who still owes money
- **Credit owing** = that total, only for customers allowed to buy on credit

**Wallet** is separate. Wallet is money the customer can spend.

### Find customers who owe money

On Hub **Customers**, filter **Owes money** and sort the list.

Open a customer to start an order or record a payment.

## 5. Cheques

### Record cheque

Use this for a real cheque.

- Sales: after **Cheque received**, or from the order
- Owner or Manager: **Payments → Cheques → Record cheque**

If you already have the paper cheque, leave **I already have this cheque** on.

If you do not have it yet, turn that off. Status is **Waiting to receive**. Outstanding does not change until you mark it received.

### Add existing cheque

Use this only for an old cheque from **before Sello**.

- Owner or Manager only
- Does not change Outstanding
- Does not create a payment
- Does not collect money
- Do not use it for a cheque a Sales Rep just received

### Cheque statuses

| Status | Meaning | Money |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting to receive** | You do not have the paper cheque yet | No change |
| **In hand** | You have the cheque | Creates a payment. Outstanding may go down, or wait for approval |
| **Given** | You handed the paper cheque to someone else | Outstanding stays as it is. You can bring it back, correct who received it, or return it to the customer |
| **Returned to customer** | You gave that cheque back to the customer | Outstanding goes back up |
| **At the bank** | You took it to the bank | Status only |
| **Bank paid** | The bank paid it | Status only |
| **Bank returned** | It bounced | Outstanding goes back up if it had been applied |
| **Cancelled** | It will not be used | Same reverse if it had been applied |

Owner or Manager moves a cheque forward: mark received, take it to the bank, then mark it paid.

On a cheque that is **In hand**, **Give to someone** records who received the paper cheque and the date. Use this when you pass a customer’s cheque to a distributor or another person instead of taking it to your bank.

On a **Given** cheque you can correct who received it. **I have it back** means you have the paper again, and Outstanding stays as it is. **Return to customer** gives the cheque back to the customer, and Outstanding goes back up.

### Where a Sales Rep cheque appears

It appears by itself under **Hub → Payments → Cheques**.

Do not add it again.

If approval is on, a cheque in hand may show **Received — needs approval**. Outstanding changes after approval.

If you skipped the cheque, open the **order** → **Record collection** → **Cheque**.

## 6. Owner / Manager — daily work

### Dashboard

A snapshot of sales, customers, outstanding, and low stock.

**Year** means this calendar year.

Tap **Outstanding** or **Low stock** to open that list.

On a computer:

- **Alt+O** starts an order
- **Alt+M** records a payment
- **Alt+C** adds a customer
- **Alt+P** adds a product

### Orders

Open an order to deliver, collect, view or print the invoice, or share it.

**Mark as delivered** finishes the remaining goods, reduces stock, and updates Outstanding for the unpaid delivered value.

**Export** downloads the current Orders list to Excel. Customers has the same **Export**.

### Correct an invoice

Owner or Manager only. Sales Reps do not see this.

Open a **Placed**, **Partially delivered**, or **Completed** order. Tap **Correct invoice**.

You can correct a paid invoice. The same invoice number and the same link stay. Do not send a new link.

Rules:

- A **Draft** uses **Edit draft**, not Correct invoice
- A cancelled or archived order cannot be corrected
- You cannot change the customer after any money has been collected or is waiting for approval
- The new total cannot be lower than money already collected, plus collections still waiting for approval
- Stock and the customer balance move by the difference only

If the corrected total is higher than what was already paid, the difference stays outstanding. Collect it with **Record collection**.

### Cancel an order

Owner or Manager can:

- **Cancel order** on a draft, or on a placed order where nothing has been delivered. Stock and the customer balance stay as they are. A shared invoice link stops opening
- **Cancel remaining** when some goods are already delivered. Delivered goods stay. Undelivered goods are closed. Stock is not changed for the cancelled units

A completed order cannot be cancelled.

Cancelling does not refund a payment that was already recorded.

### Change the Sales Rep

Owner or Manager: open the order and tap **Change Sales Rep**.

This is for reports. It does not move the money again.

A cancelled order cannot be changed.

A Sales Rep cannot do this. See [Wrong Sales Rep](#q-wrong-sales-rep).

### Customers

Add customers, set credit allowed, and add an opening balance.

### Payments

Two tabs:

- **Payments** — money received. **Receive Payment** is here. Approve or reject pending collections here
- **Cheques** — the cheque book

A yellow note means a collection is waiting for review.

**Correct payment** fixes a saved collection. Sello reverses the old one and saves the corrected one.

You cannot make the amount smaller than what is already applied to the invoices. If you enter more than those invoices, the extra goes into the customer’s wallet. The invoice allocation stays as it was.

You can also correct an opening balance, or cheque details, when your role allows it.

### Products and inventory

Products are the catalog. Inventory is stock on hand.

Sales can order above stock only if Settings allow **Allow orders beyond available stock**.

### Invoices

**View invoice** opens the customer link. **Print invoice** prints from this device.

If your business uses the compact invoice:

- more items fit on a page
- print is solid black, which suits a dot-matrix printer
- the logo can print in black
- an authorized signature can appear on every compact invoice
- the sales rep name and mobile appear together, when that person has a mobile on **Team**
- every line has a **Discount** column, even when the discount is zero

The standard invoice does not have a Discount column.

The customer link stays the same after a correction. On an iPhone, the link opens as a simple page.

Printed invoices do not repeat the browser date, title, or page count in the margin.

### Team

People, roles, and who can sign in. Put the sales rep’s mobile on their Team record if it should appear on the compact invoice.

### Settings

**Orders & Invoices**

- Whether Sales Reps can see outstanding
- Whether Sales Rep collections need approval
- Whether Sales Reps can record delivery
- What the customer invoice may show

**Inventory**

- Allow orders beyond available stock

**Notifications**

- Order and collection messages
- **SMS the buyer again when an invoice is corrected** — off unless you turn it on. It sends only if an SMS was already sent for that order. The link stays the same. A failed SMS does not undo the correction
- SMS quota, only if your company has an SMS pack

**Product Details**

- Extra product fields for your industry

## 7. Reports

The full export that is ready today is **Collections Report**.

### What “As of” means

The report answers: on this date, what was still unpaid?

It uses the end of that day. As of 31 March includes everything that happened on 31 March.

### Sales Rep filters

Tap the Sales Rep chips. An empty selection means **All Sales Reps**.

This uses the Sales Rep stored on each order.

### Opening balances

Opening-balance rows appear when you have not filtered to one Sales Rep. They are not tied to a Sales Rep.

### Export

On Collections Report: **Export PDF** and **Export Excel**.

Orders and Customers lists also have **Export** for the rows you are looking at.

## 8. Common questions

### Sales Rep

#### Wrong Sales Rep on an order {#q-wrong-sales-rep}

The order starts under the person who was signed in.

An Owner or Manager can open the order and tap **Change Sales Rep**.

A Sales Rep cannot change it.

If it is still a **Draft**, cancel it and create it again on the correct account.

Do not collect the same money twice.

#### How do I collect money?

See [How to collect money](#how-to-collect-money).

Outstanding changes when the payment is **Completed**, or when the Owner **approves** it. It does not change while the status is **Pending Review**.

#### The customer owed Rs. 2,000. I made a Rs. 3,000 credit order. Why is Outstanding still Rs. 2,000? {#q-outstanding-not-updating}

Submitting an order is not the same as delivering it.

| Step | Outstanding |
|---|---|
| Before | Rs. 2,000 |
| After **Submit order** (Credit) | Still **Rs. 2,000** |
| After **delivery** of the Rs. 3,000, still unpaid | **Rs. 5,000** |

Open the order. Check **Delivery**: Ordered, Delivered, Remaining.

If Remaining is not zero, this order is not fully on Outstanding yet.

If Sales Reps cannot record delivery, the Owner or Manager taps **Record delivery**.

#### The customer has Rs. 3,000 in the wallet. The order is Rs. 12,000. How do I use both?

Open the order → **Record collection**.

Sello fills **Apply from wallet** with Rs. 3,000.

**Amount still to receive** shows Rs. 9,000. Choose cash, card, bank, or cheque for that rest.

Save once.

### Owner / Manager

#### A Sales Rep recorded a cheque. Where is it?

**Payments → Cheques.** It appears by itself.

Do not use **Add existing cheque**.

**Waiting to receive** means Outstanding has not changed.

**In hand**, with approval on, needs approval before Outstanding changes.

#### What is Add existing cheque?

A history note for a cheque from before Sello.

It does not collect money. It does not change Outstanding.

#### Can I fix a paid invoice?

Yes. Open the order and tap **Correct invoice**.

The invoice number and the public link stay the same.

You cannot drop the total below money already collected. You cannot change the customer after a collection exists.

#### Can I cancel an order that was taken by mistake?

Yes, if nothing has been delivered. Open it and tap **Cancel order**.

If some goods were delivered, tap **Cancel remaining**. Delivered goods stay.

A completed order cannot be cancelled. Cancelling does not refund a payment.

### Short answers

**Draft vs submitted**
Draft is not a sale yet. Placed means the order is real. Delivery may still be waiting.

**Paid vs Credit vs Cheque**
Paid today means collect now. Credit means pay later. Cheque is a paper cheque, recorded on its own.

**More than stock**
Only if Settings allow it. Otherwise Sello blocks submit.

**Product options**
Choices on a product, such as size. Pick them when you add the line.

**SMS**
A failed text never undoes an order or a collection. The quota shows only if your company has an SMS pack.

## Limits in this version

These are missing on purpose for now. Do not look for a button that is not there.

### No refund

There is no **Refund** action.

- **Cancel order** does not give money back
- **Cancel remaining** does not give money back
- You cannot turn a completed collection into cash handed back to the customer
- You cannot shrink a paid invoice below the money already collected
- You cannot make a payment smaller than the amount already applied to its invoices

If the customer paid too much, the extra stays in the **wallet**. Use it on a later collection. It is not a cash refund.

A bounced or cancelled cheque puts **outstanding** back up. That is not a refund of cash.

### Money you cannot reshape

- Raising a payment above the invoices puts the extra in the wallet. The amount on each invoice stays as it was
- Growing an invoice after payment does not pull wallet in, and it does not change the old collection. The difference becomes outstanding. Collect it with **Record collection**
- Credit limit is a note. Sello does not block an order that goes over it

### By role

**Sales Rep**

- Can collect, including wallet, on their orders
- Can cancel only their own draft
- Cannot correct an invoice or a payment
- Cannot change the Sales Rep on an order
- Cannot approve a collection
- Cannot cancel a placed order
- Outstanding on Home and on the visit shows only when the company allows it

**Sales In-charge**

- Can take orders and record payments in the Hub
- Cannot approve collections
- Cannot correct an invoice, a payment, an opening balance, or a cheque
- Cannot change the Sales Rep on an order

**Store In-charge**

- Works on products, stock, and delivery
- Has no Payments page and cannot collect or refund

**Manager**

- Same daily screens as the Owner, including corrections and approvals
- Cannot change some company settings, such as billing and ownership

**Owner**

- Can correct, approve, cancel (when the rules above allow), and change the Sales Rep
- Still has no refund

### Everyone

- Orders, payments, and cheques need internet. A visit and a draft can wait on the phone
- WhatsApp opens a share on the device. Sello does not send it through a business API
- A failed SMS never undoes an order or a collection
